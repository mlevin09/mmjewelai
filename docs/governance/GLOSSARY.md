# JewelAI Glossary

Status: Working

These definitions are Working canonical terminology. Product- or architecture-specific specifications may refine them within their scope.

## Ecosystem
The overall JewelAI family of specialized products, shared capabilities, integrations, and platform foundations.

## Product
A coherent JewelAI offering addressing a defined set of user, organization, or workflow problems.

## Module
A bounded capability that may exist within a product or shared platform. A module is not automatically an independently commercialized product.

## Shared Core
Capabilities or domain foundations justified by genuine cross-product requirements. Shared does not imply one centralized service, deployment, database, or persistence layer.

## Shared Capability
A capability intentionally reusable across more than one product or workflow.

## Product-specific Capability
Logic or behavior belonging to the requirements of a particular product or workflow.

## Product State
Structured domain state describing a jewelry object and relevant attributes. It is distinct from a generated visual representation.

## AI Generation State
Technical state associated with AI generation/editing and its execution context. It is not synonymous with Product State.

## Version
A distinguishable state or revision of an artifact, object, schema, specification, or generated result.

## Current Version
The version currently active within the applicable workflow. It is not necessarily Approved.

## Approved State
A state explicitly approved within a workflow that defines approval. Generation alone does not create Approved State.

## CHANGE
Attributes or elements explicitly intended to change in a controlled operation.

## KEEP
Attributes or elements intended to remain unchanged.

## LOCKED
Attributes or constraints that must not change and should be enforced/verified through appropriate system mechanisms rather than prompt wording alone.

## Visual Identity
The set of characteristics that make a jewelry design recognizably the same intended object across relevant visual operations.

## Verification
Checks used to determine whether an output satisfies applicable requirements, constraints, preserved properties, and acceptance criteria.

## Output Profile
A set of output requirements associated with a purpose or workflow, such as exploratory concept, consultation, e-commerce, marketing, or internal review.

## Successful Scenario
A workflow scenario completed to its defined acceptance criteria, including required quality and constraint preservation.

## Cost per Successful Scenario
Total relevant execution cost divided by scenarios that satisfy the defined success criteria; more meaningful than raw cost per API call when retries/failures differ.

## User
An individual interacting with JewelAI or represented in its identity model.

## Role
A user's professional or system role within a relevant context. One user may have multiple roles.

## Organization
A company, team, workshop, brand, or other organizational entity represented in JewelAI.

## Tenant
An isolation context used where multi-tenant architecture requires it. Organization and Tenant are not assumed to be identical unless defined by architecture.

## Entitlement
A rule or grant determining access to a product/capability. It is distinct from authentication, authorization, usage allowance, metering, billing, and payment status.

## Fact
Information supported by reliable evidence.

## Decision
An explicitly recorded team choice. A proposal or recommendation is not a decision.

## Requirement
A currently applicable requirement supported by the relevant authoritative source.

## Proposal
A suggested approach not yet established as a decision.

## Recommendation
An advised course of action, not a team decision.

## Hypothesis
An assumption about users, market, product value, technology, workflow, economics, or another matter that requires validation.

## Evidence
Observed, measured, tested, researched, or otherwise supported information used to evaluate a question or hypothesis.

## Assumption
A temporary premise used to continue work when information is incomplete.

## Open Question
A material matter that remains unresolved.

## Historical
Information accurately describing an earlier state but not current by default.

## Superseded
Information explicitly replaced in whole or in part by a later source or decision.
